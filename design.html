Seven stories that defined the day — from Malacañang to the Bay of Bengal
LEAD STORY · DIPLOMACY
TRUMP MEETS MARCOS IN MANILA, THEN TO SHENZHEN
A red-carpet welcome and full military honours at Malacañang as Trump meets President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. and reaffirms the treaty alliance — then flies to China for Thursday’s first three-way summit with Xi and Putin.
MIDDLE EAST
AUSTRALIA SANCTIONS WEST BANK SETTLERS
Canberra sanctions three Israelis and four organisations over settlement expansion and settler violence — the first time a construction firm is targeted — a day after Britain sanctioned eight builders.
WEATHER · SOUTH ASIA
CYCLONE AKVAN BEARS DOWN ON INDIA
A severe cyclonic storm churns over the Bay of Bengal, winds gusting to 115 kmph, bound for Andhra Pradesh on Friday as a very severe storm, with heavy rain warnings along the east coast.
ENERGY · MIDDLE EAST
INDIA CONDEMNS HORMUZ TANKER ATTACK
New Delhi condemns the attack on the LPG tanker MT Tara Gas in the Strait of Hormuz; 21 of the 24 Indian crew are evacuated ashore at Khasab.
BUSINESS
BHP, RIO TINTO CHIEFS TO MEET CHINA’S LEADERS
Iron ore bosses head to Beijing on Friday to meet Premier Li Qiang as China’s state buyer squeezes producers; prices slip below $90 a tonne, a four-year low.
CLIMATE · BUSINESS
AMAZON’S $7BN GREEN BET ON INDIA
Amazon plans a $7 billion renewable-energy investment in India by 2030, one of its biggest clean-power bets in Asia.
INSTITUTIONS
INDIA RE-ELECTED TO ITU COUNCIL
India wins another term on the International Telecommunication Union Council, serving 2027–2030, as the UN telecom body sets the rules for a connected world.
